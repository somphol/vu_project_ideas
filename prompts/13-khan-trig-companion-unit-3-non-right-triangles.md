TASK: Build a complete, production-ready, interactive educational web application named "Khan Trig Companion: Unit 3 Non-Right Triangles Studio" using vanilla HTML5, CSS3, ES6 JavaScript, and HTML5 Canvas.

======================================================================
PROJECT BACKGROUND & PEDIGREE:
======================================================================
This application is part of an open-source digital math booklet series
modeled strictly after the Khan Academy Trigonometry curriculum
(Unit 3: Non-Right Triangles & Geometry). Its purpose is to help
students solve triangles that lack a 90-degree right angle using
the Law of Sines, the Law of Cosines, and area formulas. It runs
entirely client-side with zero external frameworks and is optimized
for GitHub Pages deployment.

======================================================================
1. MODULE ARCHITECTURE (3 CORE INTERACTIVE PLAYGROUNDS)
======================================================================

Provide a top navigation tab bar allowing seamless switching
between 3 distinct learning modules:

----------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 1: LAW OF SINES & THE AMBIGUOUS CASE (SSA) SANDBOX
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pedagogical Goal: Teach students how to use a/sin(A) = b/sin(B) = c/sin(C)
  and visualize why the ambiguous case (Side-Side-Angle) can produce
  zero, one, or two distinct triangles.
- Canvas Components:
  - Interactive triangle builder where users drag side lengths a, b,
    and angle A.
  - Dynamic Visualizer: When SSA creates two possible triangles
    (the "swinging side" paradox), render both triangle overlays in
    contrasting colors (e.g., Solid Cyan vs. Dotted Coral) to prove
    why two valid solutions exist.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 2: LAW OF COSINES (SAS & SSS) SOLVER
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pedagogical Goal: Master c² = a² + b² - 2ab cos(C) for Side-Angle-Side
  and Side-Side-Side scenarios where the Law of Sines cannot get started.
- Interactive Controls:
  - Sliders for sides a, b, and included angle C.
  - Live Step-by-Step Algebraic Substitution Drawer: Shows how the
    formula expands, computes intermediate values, and extracts the
    final unknown side or angle in real time.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3: KHAN PRACTICE SANDBOX & NAVIGATION WORD PROBLEMS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pedagogical Goal: Apply triangulation to real-world mapping,
  aviation, and surveying scenarios.
- Problem Generator Modes:
  1. "Find Missing Side/Angle using Law of Sines".
  2. "Find Missing Element using Law of Cosines".
  3. "Real-World Surveying Problem" (e.g., distance across a lake
     or tracking a ship's bearing).
- Interactive Features:
  - Input field for student answer with "Check Answer" button.
  - "💡 Show Step-by-Step Visual Solution" button: Animates the
    triangulation steps on a canvas map grid.
  - Score Streak Counter & Mastery Badge System.

======================================================================
