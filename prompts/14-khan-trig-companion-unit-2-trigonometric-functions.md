======================================================================
PROJECT BACKGROUND & PEDIGREE:
======================================================================
This application is part of an open-source digital math booklet series modeled strictly after the Khan Academy Trigonometry curriculum (Unit 2: Trigonometric Functions). Its purpose is to help students transition from static right triangles to circular motion and wave mechanics. It runs entirely client-side with zero external frameworks and is optimized for GitHub Pages.

======================================================================
1. MODULE ARCHITECTURE (3 CORE INTERACTIVE PLAYGROUNDS)
======================================================================

Provide a top navigation tab bar allowing seamless switching between 3 distinct learning modules:

----------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 1: THE UNIT CIRCLE & RADIAN MEASURE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pedagogical Goal: Demystify radians and show how coordinates on the unit circle correspond directly to cosine and sine.
- Canvas Components:
  * Interactive Unit Circle with a draggable point around the circumference.
  * Real-time readouts of Angle θ (in both Degrees and Radians, e.g., 30° = π/6).
  * Coordinate Display: (x, y) = (cos θ, sin θ) updating live.
  * Quadrant Sign Tracker: Highlights positive/negative signs for sin, cos, and tan across Quadrants I to IV.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 2: SINUSOIDAL WAVE GENERATOR & TRANSFORMATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pedagogical Goal: Connect circular motion on the unit circle to wave propagation on a Cartesian graph.
- Interactive Sliders:
  * Amplitude (A): 0.5 to 3.0 (stretches wave vertically).
  * Frequency / Period (B): Adjusts wave frequency y = A sin(Bx).
  * Phase Shift (C): Shifts wave horizontally.
  * Vertical Shift (D): Moves midline up and down.
- Canvas Animation:
  * Side-by-side or linked view showing the point rotating around the unit circle on the left, while the sine wave traces out smoothly on the right in real-time.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3: KHAN PRACTICE SANDBOX & EXACT VALUE QUIZ
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pedagogical Goal: Master exact values of trigonometric functions for special angles without a calculator.
- Quiz Modes:
  * "Evaluate Exact Value" (e.g., "What is sin(2π/3)?").
  * "Degree-to-Radian Conversion Challenge".
- Interactive Features:
  * Multiple-choice options or exact value input fields with instant feedback and step-by-step visual explanations on the unit circle.
