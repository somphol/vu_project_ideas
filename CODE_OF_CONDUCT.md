# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a useful, low-drama place to learn and build. We pledge to make participation
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and generous with the person.
- Assume good faith; ask a clarifying question before assigning a motive.
- Give actionable feedback: what, where, why, and a suggested next step.
- Respect that the briefs are intentionally verbatim and that counts are contract constants.
- Credit sources and collaborators.
- Accept a maintainer's decision gracefully; reopen with new evidence, not repetition.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or unwanted attention.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or imagery.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information (doxxing) without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact on the GitHub profile
<https://github.com/somphol>. Do not open a public issue with personal details. The maintainer will
review, may remove content, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban participation. A
report will be handled as confidentially as practical; retaliation is itself a violation.
